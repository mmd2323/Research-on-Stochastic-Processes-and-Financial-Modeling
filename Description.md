# Research on Stochastic Processes and Financial Modeling

This repository contains a research project conducted as part of a Research Assistant position.  
The project explores the mathematical foundations and financial applications of stochastic processes, focusing on Brownian motion, Itô calculus, and stochastic differential equations.

---

## Objectives
- Study stochastic processes and their properties (Markov, martingale, and diffusion processes).  
- Apply stochastic calculus to model uncertainty in financial systems.  
- Derive and simulate the Black–Scholes model using Itô’s lemma.  
- Analyze Greeks and jump–diffusion extensions for option pricing.  

---

## Contents
- **Notebooks:** Step-by-step Python notebooks illustrating stochastic processes, Brownian paths, and numerical solutions to SDEs.  
- **Report:** Theoretical explanations and mathematical derivations included in  
  `Rapport_de_recherche_final_with_references.pdf`.  

---

## Key Topics
- Brownian Motion and Wiener Processes  
- Martingales and Stopping Times  
- Itô Integral and Itô’s Lemma  
- Stochastic Differential Equations (SDEs)  
- Black–Scholes Model and Greeks  
- Jump–Diffusion Processes  

---

## Tools and Libraries
- **Python**
  - `numpy` – numerical computation  
  - `matplotlib` – visualization of stochastic paths  
  - `scipy` – numerical integration and random sampling  
  - `sympy` – symbolic differentiation and verification  
- **LaTeX** for theoretical report formatting and equation rendering  

---

## Results
The project provides:
- Python simulations of Brownian motion, SDEs, and financial diffusion models.  
- Theoretical derivations of Itô’s formula and its use in Black–Scholes option pricing.  
- An illustrated and referenced report linking mathematical rigor to financial intuition.

---

## Academic Context
This work was completed as part of ongoing research in financial mathematics and stochastic calculus.  
It forms the theoretical foundation for later projects in machine learning–based portfolio optimization and time series forecasting.
